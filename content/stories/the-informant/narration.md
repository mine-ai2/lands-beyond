# Leap In

Los Angeles. 1947. A rainy night and a phone call that brought you here.

# Scene

Rain streaks down the diner window, turning the neon sign outside into a bleeding smear of red. Rosie's All-Nite. The coffee's bitter and the pie's worse, but nobody comes here for the food.

You came because of a phone call. A man's voice, nervous, talking fast: "I know who killed Delgado. Meet me at Rosie's, midnight. Come alone."

You're not a cop. You're not even sure why you're looking into Delgado's death. Maybe because his sister asked. Maybe because nobody else would.

The man in the booth across from you matches the voice: mid-forties, thinning hair, hands that won't stay still. He's wearing a cheap suit that used to be nice. An accountant, maybe. Someone who counts other people's money.

He looks up as you slide in.
